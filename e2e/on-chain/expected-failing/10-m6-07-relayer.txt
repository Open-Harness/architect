# The relayer (M6.07) is on main and marked experimental (#784, Goal 18's slice 9). Its two replacement checks wait on a fee-bump replacement the relayer does not have yet: it sends a dropped transaction again, byte for byte (design/later.md). The 100-concurrent check fails intermittently under load (1 in 7 runs on 2 cores, 1 in 4 on 1 core; failed on CI's first run): reconcile settles rows from a stale snapshot and ends a row whose relay finished mid-loop as lost, so a mined transaction reads as dropped (Snag #1110). Each comes off when its fix turns it green.
