handle derived

A public name that belongs to one person until they release it, and is the address of a public page.

Route
/things/handle · typed link links.handle()
Opens from
home (the breadcrumb: home › handle)
Who may open it
anyone
Shows
No read. A derived page names none.
Offers
  • handle.claimsigned-in

    Claim a free name and make it the address of your public page, with its profile.

  • handle.releaserole handle.handleOwner

    Free your name and take its public page offline. Anyone may claim the name afterwards.

  • handle.resolveanyone

    Find the handle that holds a claimed name. A released or unclaimed name has none.

Components
None.
