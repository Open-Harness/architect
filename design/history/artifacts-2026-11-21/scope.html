1. What Orthograph is

You describe a system once, in a typed blueprint, and everything else falls out of it: the server, the SDK, the CLI, MCP tools, the docs, the portal, telemetry and the proof. "The only code you write by hand is behaviour." Journeys say what the app must do, and ortho prove judges the app against them from real evidence, so a coding agent can climb them until they are green.

What it is not: two products, a fork of agent-native, or (today) anything in the cloud or on public npm.
