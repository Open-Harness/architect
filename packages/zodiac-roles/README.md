# @orthograph/zodiac-roles

The Zodiac Roles 2.1.1 adapter (#1163; unit A4 of `design/decisions/CX-safe.md` section 4). An Org Budget is a Roles
role that the company's Safe grants, used by a member through `execTransactionWithRole` and scoped to one function with
an allowance (`design/decisions/CX-bridge-rails.md`, "Who signs: every payout is on a budget"). It is hand-written to the
shape `ortho new adapter --from-abi` will generate (#1164) and is regenerated by it once that lands.

- `Roles.deploy({ safe, saltNonce })`: the modifier through Zodiac's `ModuleProxyFactory`, with the Safe as owner,
  avatar and target, and its address worked out before it is deployed.
- `Roles.budgetCalls(modifier, budget)`: the Org Budget compiled to the direct calls on the modifier that the Safe makes:
  the role assigned, the target and the one function scoped (the amount `and(lteUint256(limit), withinAllowance(key))`,
  the recipients one of a list or anyone), and `setAllowance`.
- `Roles.execTransactionWithRole(modifier, ...)`: a member's call through its role.
- `Roles.allowance(port, modifier, key)` and `Roles.accrued(allowance, now)`: the allowance as stored, and the balance
  accrued so far.
- `Roles.refusals` and `Roles.refusalOf(error)`: the modifier's errors for the chain port, and a refusal typed
  (`ConditionViolation` with its `status` by name, `NoMembership`, `NotAuthorized`, ...).
- `ZodiacWorld.install(rpcUrl)`: the world starter. The factory and the Roles 2.1.1 mastercopy, with its libraries, at
  their canonical addresses on a world's Anvil, with their canonical code.

Status: the handlers are empty (`Effect.die`), and `roles.test.ts`, `budget.test.ts` and `world.test.ts` are the checks,
listed in `expected-failing/` until the build lands. The Anvil checks are skipped unless `ORTHO_CHAIN_EVM_ANVIL=1`, as
chain-evm's are; CI's `check-static` sets it.
